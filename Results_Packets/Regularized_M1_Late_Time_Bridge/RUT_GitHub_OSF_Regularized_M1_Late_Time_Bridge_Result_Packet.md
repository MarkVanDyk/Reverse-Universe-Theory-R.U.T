## Canonical summary statement

Within the present BAO+growth pipeline, the regularized M1 late-time bridge is best interpreted as a **late-time correction sector** rather than a universal all-redshift modification. In its healthiest anchored form, it remains **statistically competitive** with ΛCDM, develops a **broad stable basin** instead of a narrow optimizer spike, and shows a **repeatable observational fingerprint** concentrated in radial BAO structure and selected growth points.

## Executive result

The strongest current empirical reading is not that the bridge has decisively replaced ΛCDM, but that it has matured into a serious late-time competitor under regularization. The current best region sits near:

- **d_G ≈ 0.18–0.19**
- **δa ≈ 0.04–0.05**
- **a\* = 0.50**
- **z_cut ≈ 0.8–1.0** as the strongest anchored global regime
- **near-competitive full-fit performance**, with the strongest anchored case at **ΔBIC ≈ +0.390** for **z_cut = 0.8**

The main empirical strength is a persistent **growth-to-geometry transfer signal**. The main unresolved weakness is **low-z to high-z extrapolation**, especially when pushed into higher-redshift BAO structure.

## Development arc

### 1. Stage C — signal without sufficient health

Stage C established that a bridge-coupled version could materially reduce raw χ², but only in forms that were too flexible to treat as healthy. Baseline sat near **χ²_total ≈ 15.79** and **BIC ≈ 27.77**. M1C lowered χ²_total to about **12.64** but paid a heavier BIC penalty, and M2C pushed raw fit lower still while remaining too costly in complexity. That made Stage C diagnostically useful, but not yet persuasive as a disciplined physical result.

### 2. Stage D — regularization preserved part of the signal

Stage D mattered because the signal survived tightening. Baseline remained **χ²_total ≈ 15.79** and **BIC ≈ 27.77**. M1D settled near **d_G ≈ 0.1887**, with **χ²_total ≈ 13.18** and **BIC ≈ 34.15**. M2D still reduced χ² more aggressively, but the simpler M1 family emerged as the healthier path once complexity cost was included.

### 3. Stage G — the bridge became a real competing candidate

Stage G is the turning point where the regularized M1 bridge stopped looking like a lucky optimizer point. A fixed-width scan produced a smooth band of near-tied outcomes: **ΔBIC ≈ +0.405 at δa = 0.04**, **+0.420 at 0.05**, **+0.442 at 0.06**, and still only **+0.642 by 0.12**. Meanwhile, the fitted cosmology moved very little across the competitive region. That is the clearest evidence so far for a coherent basin rather than a fragile spike.

### 4. Stage I — mixed hold-out behavior, but one strong asymmetry

Stage I showed that the bridge is not uniformly more predictive than ΛCDM, but it is also not trivial. When trained on BAO and tested on growth, it was slightly worse. When trained on growth and tested on BAO, it was dramatically better. When trained on low-z and tested on high-z, it weakened. The cleanest summary is that the model has a real **growth-to-geometry transfer advantage**, but not yet full redshift-general robustness.

### 5. Stage J — late-time interpretation became the cleanest reading

Stage J sharpened the model class. At **z_cut = 0.6**, the full-fit late-time bridge gave **ΔBIC ≈ +1.166**, while the low-z → high-z hold-out penalty improved somewhat. At **z_cut = 0.8**, the full-fit case improved to **ΔBIC ≈ +0.390**, currently the strongest anchored full-fit result. At **z_cut = 1.0**, the full-fit remained near-tied at **ΔBIC ≈ +0.403**. This points to a real tradeoff: **z_cut ≈ 0.8–1.0** gives the best global competitiveness, while **z_cut ≈ 0.6** is the safer conservative late-time version.

### 6. Stage L — the late-time basin is broad

The basin scan strengthens the late-time reading. For **δa = 0.04**, the sequence moved from clearly worse at **z_cut = 0.5** to competitive at **z_cut = 0.8**, **1.0**, and **1.2**. For **δa = 0.05**, the same pattern persisted, with the strongest anchored full-fit case again sitting near **z_cut = 0.8**. The result is no longer best described as a one-point accident. It is better described as a **broad competitive late-time basin** centered roughly around **δa ≈ 0.04–0.05** and **z_cut ≈ 0.8–1.2**.
